Tap a user's avatar from a conversation or contact list to open the account info page.

## Profile Card

Avatar, nickname, username, ID, remark name.

## Conversation History

Shows all Grix conversations with this user. Supports search.

## Actions

- **+Conversation** — Start a new conversation thread
- **Set Remark** — Only visible to you
- **Add Friend** — Shown when not yet friends
- **Forward Contact Card** — Send the profile card to another conversation
- **Report User** / **Delete Friend**

## Group Profile Page

Tap the group name in a group chat: Member list, role badges, group management entry.

## Group Invitation Page

Open via group QR code or link: Group owner info, member count, Join / Enter group chat.

## Service Region Selection

When using Grix for the first time, select your service region:

| Region | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Mainland China | Data stored on servers in mainland China |
| Global | Data stored on global servers |

> After switching regions, you'll need to obtain a new verification code.

## Sign Up for a Grix Account

Grix uses email registration. The process is:

1. Click "Sign Up" on the login page
2. Enter your email address
3. Complete the CAPTCHA verification
4. Click "Send Code" — Grix will send a 6-digit verification code to your email
5. Enter the email verification code
6. Set a password
7. Read and agree to the App Agreement and User Agreement
8. Click "Sign Up" to complete

## Log In to Grix

### Password Login

1. Enter your username or email
2. Enter your password
3. Click "Log In"

**Convenience feature**: Remember your username and password for auto-fill next time.

### Google Sign-In

Click the "Google Sign-In" button and authorize through your Google account. Supported on iOS, Android, and Web.

### Apple Sign-In

Click the "Sign in with Apple" button and authorize using your Apple ID. Supported on iOS and macOS.

### QR Code Login (Desktop / Web)

1. Click "Show QR Login" on the desktop or web client
2. Scan the QR code with a logged-in Grix mobile client
3. Confirm the login request on your mobile device
4. The desktop client automatically completes login

**Status flow**: Waiting for scan → Scanned, waiting for confirmation → Confirmed, logging in

> QR codes have an expiration time. If expired, click "Refresh QR Code" to generate a new one.

## Forgot Password / Reset Password

1. Click "Forgot Password" on the Grix login page
2. Enter your registered email
3. Complete the CAPTCHA verification
4. Get the email verification code
5. Set a new password and confirm
6. Click "Reset Password"

After resetting, you'll need to log in again with the new password.

## App Agreement and User Agreement

Grix requires users to read and agree to the following before signing up or first login:

- **App Agreement** — Product positioning, communication risk notices, and usage boundaries
- **User Agreement** — Service rules, rights and obligations, and liability boundaries

## Login Language Switching

The Grix login page supports real-time language switching for multilingual users.
